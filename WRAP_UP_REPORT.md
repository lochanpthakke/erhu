## Proposed licensing position

[LICENSE](LICENSE) is a concise **source-visible proprietary draft** for rights actually controlled in original code, artwork, branding and other protected expression. It permits inspection and authorized personal binary use, reserving copying/reuse, modification, rebranding, redistribution and commercial exploitation subject to existing grants and law. Lawful independent implementation of general features remains permitted.

Original application-specific UI/design expression is included only where controlled; common patterns, algorithms, equalizers, effects, transitions, mixing, playlists and library/player features are not claimed exclusively. All third-party and user-owned content is excluded from proprietary ownership claims regardless of APK/repository inclusion, online retrieval or display. Exclusion does not confer missing rights or remove applicable upstream/reused-GPL obligations.

The draft cannot revoke prior GPL or component/copyleft rights. It cannot override GitHub's own grants or prevent technical cloning/forking. The current [GitHub terms §D](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service) cover public-repository use and applicable hosting/processing/AI permissions. These require human review; a proprietary repository licence does not promise absolute exclusivity. No platform terms or account settings were changed or accepted here.
